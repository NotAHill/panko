## Evidence

Each item comes with evidence Panko looked up for it, from these sources:
{{sources}}

Use it in that order and cite what you used in `evidence`, short and specific (a word-list file and
its gloss, "corpus: 37 sentences", a book and line number with the form found, a URL). Attestation in the word lists or the
corpus with a matching meaning, or in a reference book, is good evidence the item is real and mainstream.
{{#web_search}}
For items the given evidence doesn't settle, and for every regional or register claim it doesn't support, you may
search the web: dialect dictionaries and papers, Wiktionary, forums where native speakers compare words. At most
{{web_searches}} searches per item.{{/web_search}}
Prefer evidence over memory, and say in `evidence` when a judgement rests on memory alone.
